MyVoteHub: event voting for Ghana

MyVoteHub lets any event company run public voting for awards, pageants and reality shows. Fans vote with MoMo, a card or a USSD code, judges score in secret, and the organiser gets paid out from their wallet.

Web, Android, and USSD on any phoneStatus: built and live; payments in test mode

Why it exists

Event voting in Ghana is big business: music awards, campus awards, pageants and TV reality shows all charge fans per vote. Most organisers still use a hired developer's one-off site, a WhatsApp poll or a manual MoMo number. The result is disputed counts, slow payouts and no audit trail.

MyVoteHub is one shared platform that any organiser can sign up to. It collects the money, counts every vote the same way across all channels, keeps judges' scores sealed until the reveal, and shows everyone a live count they can trust. MyVoteHub earns a commission on paid votes.

How an event runs

  1. Sign up. The organiser creates a company account. It waits until a MyVoteHub admin approves it.
  2. Build the event. A step-by-step builder covers the details, categories, nominees (pasted as a list), voting type and the public/judge weighting such as 70/30.
  3. Approval. The admin approves the event, and the organiser opens voting.
  4. Promotion. Nominees share their link, QR or USSD code (e.g. "dial the code, enter AOY-03").
  5. Voting. Votes arrive from web, app and USSD into one count. The organiser's dashboard updates live.
  6. Rounds. For reality shows, the organiser closes a round, evicts the lowest nominees, and chooses whether votes reset or carry over.
  7. Results. Judge scores are unsealed and combined with public votes by the chosen weighting. Results go on a full-screen reveal for the live show, with 3rd, 2nd and then the winner.
  8. Payout. The organiser requests a withdrawal to MoMo or bank, and the admin approves it.

Features

Voting options

  • Paid voting with vote bundles, e.g. 5 votes for GH₵5 or 100 for GH₵80
  • Free polls with a phone code, limited per voter (once per event or once a day)
  • Judges' secret scores blended with public votes
  • Eviction rounds for reality shows

Three ways to vote

  • Web, with no download needed
  • Android app
  • USSD on any phone, no data needed: by nominee code or by browsing menus, plus "check my votes"

Voters and nominees see one combined total. Only organisers see the split by channel.

For organisers

  • Live dashboard, vote log and payments, with CSV export
  • Wallet with earnings and withdrawals
  • USSD settings per event: bundles, greeting, on or off
  • Team management and judge assignment

For MyVoteHub admins

  • Approve, suspend, activate, deactivate or delete any account
  • Approve companies and events, and set each company's commission
  • Decide withdrawals, view all transactions and the audit log
  • Edit the USSD menu wording, pin or hide events on USSD
  • Reset forgotten passwords; switch test mode

How the money works

Voters pay MyVoteHub by MoMo or card through Hubtel. Each payment is split straight away: MyVoteHub keeps its commission and the rest goes to the organiser's wallet. Commission is set per company (10% by default, maximum 50%). There are no setup fees and no withdrawal fees.

Trust and fairness

  • Approval first: no company, team member, judge or event goes live without a MyVoteHub admin.
  • Counts come from the server: votes are recorded only after payment is confirmed, so nobody can add votes from their browser.
  • Sealed judging: each judge sees only their own scores, and organisers never see raw scores.
  • Free-vote limits: codes expire after 10 minutes and allow 5 tries. Limits apply per phone number, and sudden vote bursts are flagged.
  • Receipts and audit log: every vote has a reference, and every admin action is logged.
  • Organisers can't change their own commission, status or verification.
  • Privacy: voter phone numbers are masked, and there are a privacy policy and terms written with Ghana's Data Protection Act (Act 843) in mind.
